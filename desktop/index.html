Your LibreNMS, right on your desktop.

Know the moment something on your network needs you. DashyNMS sits quietly in the corner of your screen, tells you when an alert fires, and opens into your whole network when you want to dig in.

Download for Windows See how it works

Windows 10 and 11 · No admin rights needed · No account, nothing tracked

Up and running in a minute

Nothing to install on your server. If you can sign in to LibreNMS, you can use DashyNMS.

Install

Download the installer and run it. It installs just for you, so you don’t need admin rights.

Sign in

Enter your LibreNMS address and choose Sign in with LibreNMS. You log in on your server’s usual page and DashyNMS makes its own API token. You can paste a token instead if you’d rather.

Get on with your day

Close the window and DashyNMS keeps watching from the notification area, and lets you know when something changes.

No server to hand? Choose Try the demo when you first open it, and look around an example network.

Notifications and the tray

You don’t need LibreNMS open in a browser tab all day. DashyNMS checks it for you and only speaks up when something changes.

  • A Windows notification when an alert fires or clears, with Acknowledge right on it.
  • Pick which alerts notify you. Leave out a noisy rule everywhere or on one device, or only hear about the rules you name.
  • Each severity can fade away, stay until you dismiss it, or keep sounding. Quiet hours cover the times you’re off.
  • The tray icon turns amber or red with the number of open alerts. Click it for the latest ones without opening the app.

Know what’s wrong, and why

Every open alert in one place, with the reason it fired in plain view.

  • Open any alert to see exactly why it fired, and how many times it’s fired on that device in the last 30 days.
  • Acknowledge it with a note, so your team knows you’re on it.
  • Filter by severity, device group, rule or device, search, and export the list to a spreadsheet.
  • For admins: a full alert rule and template editor, as on the LibreNMS website, with the devices, groups and locations each rule covers.

Your whole network in one list

Find any device by name, address or location, and keep the ones you care about at the top.

  • Pin the devices you check most, so they stay at the top whatever you sort or filter by.
  • Choose your columns and sort by anything: status, address, uptime, location.
  • Act on several at once: pin them, add them to a group, put them into maintenance, or ask LibreNMS to check them again.
  • Add new devices without leaving the app, one at a time or from a pasted list or CSV.

Everything about a device, on one screen

Open any device to see whether it’s healthy, how long it’s been up, and what it’s connected to.

  • Status, uptime and response times at a glance, with graphs over time.
  • Ports, sensors and neighbours, so you can see what’s plugged into what. Follow a neighbour and use back and forward to retrace your steps.
  • The detail when you need it: CPU, memory and disk, VLANs, MAC and ARP tables, inventory, routing, PoE, wireless and a searchable event log.
  • Connect straight to it over SSH, Telnet or the web, or put it into maintenance before you work on it.

See how it all connects

Maps of your network that draw themselves from what LibreNMS already knows.

  • A network map drawn from each device’s neighbours, and a geographical map of your sites.
  • Maps of your own, laid out however makes sense to you.
  • Health brings temperature, fan, optical power and signal readings from across your network into one place.

And the rest

The dashboard, your logs, the other tools you use, and what you need to run it.

The dashboard

Pick widgets for alerts, devices, sensors, graphs, the busiest ports and the event log, then drag and resize them. New to it? Start from the ready-made layout.

Logs

The Logs tab has LibreNMS’s event log and alert log for your whole network. Search them, filter by device or time, and double-click an entry to open its device.

Graylog and Unimus

Connect Graylog to see its messages next to your LibreNMS logs. Connect Unimus to view a device’s config backups, compare two, or take a new one.

A backup address

Give LibreNMS a second address. If the main one stops answering, DashyNMS switches over and shows an amber icon until you switch back.

Themes and updates

Dark, light or whatever Windows is using, with your choice of accent colour. New versions download in the background and install with one click.

What you need

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11, and a LibreNMS server you can reach with its API turned on. Sign in with LibreNMS needs LibreNMS 26.4 or later over https; otherwise paste an API token.

Your data stays yours

DashyNMS talks to your own servers, and to GitHub for updates. There’s no DashyNMS account, no analytics and no cloud in the middle.

Read the privacy policy

Your token is locked to you

It’s encrypted for your Windows account on this computer only, and you can choose not to save it at all.

Everything in one folder

Your settings and token live under %APPDATA%\DashyNMS. Delete the folder and it’s gone.

Talks only to your servers

Your LibreNMS server, plus Graylog and Unimus if you set them up. It also fetches updates from GitHub and map backgrounds from OpenStreetMap.

No admin rights needed

It installs just for your Windows account, and keeps everything there too.
