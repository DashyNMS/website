Your LibreNMS, on your desktop.

Monitors your network from the Windows notification area, alerts you when something breaks, and opens your devices, maps and logs in one app.

Download for Windows See how it works

Windows 10 and 11 · No admin rights needed · No account · No tracking

Up and running in a minute

Nothing to install on your server. If you can sign in to LibreNMS, you can use DashyNMS.

Install

Download the installer and run it. It installs for your Windows account only, so you do not need admin rights.

Sign in

Enter your LibreNMS address and choose Sign in with LibreNMS. Log in on your server's usual page and DashyNMS creates its own API token, or paste a token instead.

Keep watching in the tray

Close the window and DashyNMS keeps polling from the notification area, notifying you when alert state changes.

No server to hand? Choose Try the demo on sign-in to explore an example network.

Notifications and the tray

Close the LibreNMS browser tab. DashyNMS polls for you and notifies you when something changes.

  • Get a Windows notification when an alert fires or clears, with Acknowledge right on the toast.
  • Pick which alerts notify you. Mute a noisy rule globally or on one device, or allow only the rules you name.
  • Set each severity to fade, stay until dismissed or keep sounding, and schedule quiet hours when you are off duty.
  • The tray icon turns amber or red with the open-alert count. Click it to read the latest alerts without opening the main window.

Know what is wrong, and why

Every open alert in one list, with the rule and values that tripped it.

  • Open an alert to see why it fired and how many times it fired on that device in the last 30 days.
  • Acknowledge it with a note so your team sees you are handling it.
  • Filter by severity, device group, rule or device, search, and export the list to a spreadsheet.
  • Admins get a full alert rule and template editor showing the devices, groups and locations each rule covers.

Your whole network in one list

Find any device by name, address or location, and pin the ones you check most.

  • Pin key devices so they stay at the top regardless of sort or filter.
  • Choose your columns and sort by status, address, uptime or location.
  • Act on several devices at once to pin them, add them to a group, schedule maintenance or trigger a poll.
  • Add new devices from the app, one at a time or from a pasted list or CSV.

Every device detail on one screen

Open any device to check its status, uptime, graphs and neighbour links.

  • Status, uptime and response times with history graphs.
  • Ports, sensors and neighbours with back and forward navigation as you follow links.
  • CPU, memory and disk, VLANs, MAC and ARP tables, inventory, routing, PoE, wireless and a searchable event log.
  • Connect directly over SSH, Telnet or the web, or put the device into maintenance before working on it.

See how it all connects

Topology and site maps generated from your LibreNMS data.

  • A network map built from neighbour links, plus a geographical map of your sites.
  • Custom maps you can arrange to match your network layout.
  • Health gathers temperature, fan, optical power and signal readings across your network into one view.

Dashboard, logs and integrations

Custom layouts, network logs, Graylog and Unimus integrations, and system requirements.

Customisable dashboard

Add widgets for alerts, devices, sensors, graphs, busiest ports and event logs, then drag and resize them, or start from the default layout.

Logs

Search LibreNMS event and alert logs across your network, filter by device or time, and double-click an entry to open its device.

Graylog and Unimus

Connect Graylog to view its messages alongside LibreNMS logs. Connect Unimus to read config backups, compare versions or trigger a new backup.

Backup server address

Configure a second LibreNMS address. If the primary stops responding, DashyNMS fails over and shows an amber indicator until you switch back.

Themes and updates

Dark, light or Windows system theme with your choice of accent colour. Updates download in the background and install in one click.

Requirements

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11, and a reachable LibreNMS server with its API enabled. Sign in with LibreNMS needs LibreNMS 26.4 or later over HTTPS; otherwise paste an API token.

Your data stays yours

DashyNMS talks to your own servers and to GitHub for updates. There is no DashyNMS account, no analytics and no cloud in the middle.

Read the privacy policy

Token encrypted per user

Windows encrypts your token for your account on this PC only, or you can choose not to save it.

Everything in one folder

Settings and saved tokens live in %APPDATA%\DashyNMS. Delete the folder to remove them.

Direct server connections

Connects to your LibreNMS server, plus Graylog and Unimus if configured. Also fetches updates from GitHub and map tiles from OpenStreetMap.

No admin rights needed

Installs for your Windows account only and keeps all files there.
