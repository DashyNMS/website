DashyNMS MCP

Claude and other MCP clients

Ask about your LibreNMS network. Let Claude or any MCP client answer questions from LibreNMS, with evidence beside every answer and no write access.

  • Health, alerts, ports and neighbours in plain English
  • Config diffs and logs from Unimus and Graylog
  • Read-only, with secrets redacted first

One engine for all three.

Use one on its own, or run all three together. They share the same engine, so counts and status always match.

Works with your LibreNMS server

Nothing to install on the server. If you can sign in to LibreNMS, you can use DashyNMS.

Acknowledge once, everywhere

Acknowledge an alert on your phone and your team sees it in LibreNMS and on Desktop.

Consistent results everywhere

Desktop, Mobile and MCP calculate health, alerts and neighbours the same way.

Private by design.

DashyNMS talks directly to your own servers. There is no DashyNMS account, no analytics and no cloud in the middle.

Desktop privacy policy · Mobile privacy policy · MCP privacy policy

No account

Sign in with your LibreNMS address and an API token.

Local token storage

Windows encrypts your token for your account, Mobile keeps it in the iPhone keychain or Android keystore, and MCP reads it from your local settings.

No tracking

No analytics and no advertising anywhere.

Direct server connections

Connects to your LibreNMS server, plus Graylog or Unimus if you configure them. Desktop also fetches updates from GitHub and map tiles from OpenStreetMap.
