Private by design.

DashyNMS talks to your own servers. There’s no DashyNMS account, no analytics and no cloud in the middle.

Desktop privacy policy · Mobile privacy policy

No account to create

Sign in with your LibreNMS address and an API token. That’s it.

Your token stays on your device

Encrypted for your Windows account, or kept in the iPhone keychain or Android keystore.

Nothing tracked

No analytics and no advertising, in either app.

Talks only to your servers

Your LibreNMS server, plus Graylog or Unimus if you connect them. Desktop also fetches its updates from GitHub and map tiles from OpenStreetMap.
