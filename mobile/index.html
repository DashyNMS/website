Alerts, Intelligence and your network map.

No server to hand? Choose Try the demo on the sign-in screen to try an example network.

Everything you check at your desk, on your phone.

Sign in with your LibreNMS address and an API token, or scan the sign-in QR code from DashyNMS Desktop and confirm on your phone.

Customisable dashboard

Arrange your dashboard with open alerts, down devices, items needing attention and pinned devices.

Acknowledge alerts anywhere

Swipe to acknowledge an alert with a note so your team sees you are handling it. Filter by severity or export the list.

Device search and detail

Search by name, address, location or group. Check device state, uptime, ports, sensors and neighbours.

See why an alert fired

Each alert shows the rule condition and the values that tripped it. On LibreNMS 26.10, alert groups combine faults by device and rule with details and history for each fault.

Ask on your device

Ask about your network in plain English with Apple Intelligence on supported devices. It only reads. Start a chat from a device, alert, sensor or port, and export the conversation as Markdown or PDF.

Offline last-known view

No signal or VPN? View the last data the app fetched, marked with its last update time. It refreshes once your server answers again.

Logs and config backups

Add Event log and Graylog cards to your dashboard. With Unimus configured, read and search a device's config backups, compare two versions and share them.

Notifications and widgets

Get a notification when an alert fires or clears, see the open-alert count on the app icon, and add home- or lock-screen widgets.

Device actions

Put a device into maintenance before working on it, ask LibreNMS to poll it again, or open SSH, Telnet or the web UI from the app.

More on screen on iPad.

On iPad and wider screens, the dashboard arranges alerts, pinned sensors, traffic graphs and pinned devices into columns.

  • Multi-column dashboard. View traffic graphs, pinned sensors and alert counts side by side.
  • Network Search. Trace an IP address, subnet, MAC address or hostname to its switch port and routers.
  • Offline user guide. Search the guide in Settings with no connection, or open it from alerts and Intelligence.

Private by design.

DashyNMS talks to your LibreNMS server, plus Graylog and Unimus if you connect them. On supported devices, Apple Intelligence answers on the device itself. There is no DashyNMS account, no cloud in the middle, and no tracking.

Read the privacy policy

Your token stays on your phone

The app stores your API token in the iPhone keychain or Android keystore, readable only on that device.

Widgets never hold credentials

Home- and lock-screen widgets show the last alert check's results, never your API token.

Private on the lock screen

Notifications and lock-screen widgets can hide alert details until you unlock.

Credentials go to your server only

If your server redirects the app elsewhere, your token does not follow, and the app warns you before signing in over an unencrypted connection.
