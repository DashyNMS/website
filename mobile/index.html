Private by design.

DashyNMS talks to your LibreNMS server and nothing else. There’s no DashyNMS account, no cloud in the middle, and nothing is tracked.

Read the privacy policy

Your token stays on your phone

Your API token is kept in the iPhone keychain or Android keystore, readable only on this device.

Widgets never hold it

Home- and lock-screen widgets show the last alert check’s results, never your credentials.

Discreet on the lock screen

Notifications and lock-screen widgets can keep alert details hidden until you unlock.

Credentials go to your server only

A redirect to anywhere else never carries your token, and plain http gets a clear warning before you sign in.

One engine, desk and pocket.

The app runs on the same engine as DashyNMS desktop: the same way of talking to LibreNMS, and the same alert logic. Pins, recent devices, dashboard layout and notification rules work the way they do on desktop, and a fix in one lands in both.
