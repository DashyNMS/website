No account to create · Talks only to your server · Nothing tracked

Built for on-call and on-site.

Real screens from Mobile 2.0. No server to hand? Choose Try the demo on sign-in to explore an example network.

Everything you check at your desk, sized for your hand.

Sign in with your LibreNMS address and an API token, or scan the sign-in QR code from DashyNMS Desktop and confirm on your phone.

See what matters first

A dashboard you arrange: open alerts, devices that are down, what needs attention and the devices you’ve pinned.

Alerts, dealt with anywhere

Swipe to acknowledge an alert with a note, so your team knows you’re on it. Filter by severity, or export the list.

Every device, one tap away

Search by name, address, location or group. See whether it’s up, how long it’s been running, and its ports, sensors and neighbours, with more on screen at once on iPad.

Know exactly why it fired

Each alert shows what its rule was looking for and the values that tripped it. With LibreNMS 26.10, alert groups bring faults together by device and rule, with details and history for each fault.

Ask on your device

Ask about your network in plain words with Apple Intelligence on supported devices. It only reads. Start a chat from a device, alert, sensor or port, and share the conversation as Markdown or PDF.

Keep your last view offline

No signal or VPN? See the last data the app received, clearly marked with its update time. It catches up when your server answers again.

Logs and backups, close at hand

Add Event log and Graylog cards to your dashboard. With Unimus configured, read and search a device’s config backups, compare two versions and share them.

Notifications and widgets

A notification when an alert fires or clears, the open-alert count on the app icon, and widgets for the home and lock screen.

Take action on the go

Put a device into maintenance before you work on it, ask LibreNMS to check it again, or connect to it straight from the app.

More on screen on iPad.

The dashboard expands into columns on iPad and wider displays, putting alerts, pinned sensors, traffic graphs and devices side by side.

  • Multi-column dashboard with live traffic graphs, pinned sensors and alert counts together.
  • Network Search traces any IP address, subnet, MAC address or hostname to its switch port and routers.
  • Built-in user guide works offline in Settings, with search and direct links from alerts and Intelligence.

Private by design.

DashyNMS talks to your LibreNMS server, plus Graylog and Unimus if you connect them. Intelligence answers on your device where Apple Intelligence is available. There’s no DashyNMS account, no cloud in the middle, and nothing is tracked.

Read the privacy policy

Your token stays on your phone

Your API token is kept in the iPhone keychain or Android keystore, readable only on this device.

Widgets never hold it

Home- and lock-screen widgets show the last alert check’s results, never your credentials.

Discreet on the lock screen

Notifications and lock-screen widgets can keep alert details hidden until you unlock.

Credentials go to your server only

If your server sends the app somewhere else, your token doesn’t go with it, and you’re warned before signing in over an unencrypted connection.
