This is the source for [dashynms.pckp.net](https://dashynms.pckp.net/), the website for both DashyNMS apps. Know the moment something on your network breaks, and deal with it from your desk or your phone.

## What's on the site

**DashyNMS Desktop**
The Windows app for LibreNMS: what it does, how to get started, its [release notes](https://dashynms.pckp.net/desktop/releases/), [help and support](https://dashynms.pckp.net/desktop/support/) and [privacy policy](https://dashynms.pckp.net/desktop/privacy/).

**DashyNMS Mobile**
The app for iPhone and iPad: what it does, its [release notes](https://dashynms.pckp.net/mobile/releases/), [help and support](https://dashynms.pckp.net/mobile/support/) and [privacy policy](https://dashynms.pckp.net/mobile/privacy/).

**Always up to date**
Release notes and version numbers come straight from each app's GitHub releases, so the site shows a new version as soon as it's published.

## The apps

- **DashyNMS Desktop:** [github.com/DashyNMS/desktop](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop)
- **DashyNMS Mobile:** [github.com/DashyNMS/mobile](https://github.com/DashyNMS/mobile)

## Private by design

The site has no analytics, no cookies and no tracking. The only thing it fetches from anywhere else is release notes from GitHub.

## Feedback

Spotted a mistake on the site? Email [dashynms@pckp.net](mailto:dashynms@pckp.net). For a problem with one of the apps, open an issue on [Desktop](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop/issues) or [Mobile](https://github.com/DashyNMS/mobile/issues).
